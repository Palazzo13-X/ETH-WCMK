# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our purpose

ETH-WCMK is a normative model of agency. Disagreement about the model is
productive and expected; disrespect is not. We welcome critique from ethics,
law, computer science and the domains the corpus covers (health, education,
finance, public administration, security).

## Standards

- Critique the artefact, not the person: cite the file, the test, the number.
- Separate clearly (a) what the code computes, (b) what the ontology asserts,
  (c) what a normative panel assumed.
- Report reproduction failures with the manifest, the git revision and the
  environment. "It does not reproduce" without those is not actionable.
- No harassment, no ad hominem, no dismissiveness toward domain expertise.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md`). A maintainer who receives a
report documents it, gives the person a chance to respond, and may issue a
warning, a temporary exclusion from discussion, or removal from the repository.
Appeals are handled by the other maintainer.
